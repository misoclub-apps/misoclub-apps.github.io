Account and Data Deletion

App: きょうの体重 - ダイエットに便利な超シンプルな体重記録！ (Today's Weight) — Developer: misoclub / Last updated: August 3, 2026

Every feature of this app works without signing in. If you have not signed in, your records are stored only on your device and no account or data exists on our servers. Deleting the app removes that data from your device.

If you have signed in with a Google or Apple account to use cloud sync, you can delete your account and data as follows.

Option 1: Delete from within the app (recommended)

  1. Open the app
  2. Go to the “Settings” tab
  3. Under “Cloud sync,” tap “Delete account”
  4. Review the confirmation screen and confirm the deletion

You will be offered the chance to export a backup file before the deletion goes through.

Option 2: Request deletion by email (if you already uninstalled the app)

If you have already uninstalled the app and cannot use the steps above, please contact us by email.

  • To: misoclubsupport@gmail.com
  • Subject: “Today's Weight — Account deletion request”
  • Body: include the email address you signed in with. Please send the request from that address so we can verify it is you.

We will complete the deletion within 30 days of receiving your request and reply to confirm once it is done.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes the following from our servers:

  • Your account (the user identifier and email address associated with your Google/Apple sign-in)
  • The cloud copy of your records: weight and body fat percentage, meal notes, exercise notes, and short memos
  • The cloud copy of some settings: height, target weight, goal direction, starting-weight baseline date, weight unit, and the trend line setting
  • Bookkeeping data used for syncing

Deleted data cannot be restored. There is no retention period — data is removed promptly, including from backups.

What is not deleted

  • Records on your current device. When deleting from within the app, you choose whether to also delete them (by default they are kept). If you request deletion by email, we can only act on data on our servers — remove device data by uninstalling the app or using “Delete all data” inside the app.
  • Records on your other devices. They remain on those devices. Syncing stops and the app continues to work there as a device-only app.
  • Data written to Apple Health / Health Connect. This is stored by the operating system. Data that this app wrote can be deleted from the app’s settings.
  • Backup files you exported yourself. These are under your control.
  • In-app purchase history. This is held by the App Store / Google Play and cannot be deleted by us.
