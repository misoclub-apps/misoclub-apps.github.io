Privacy Policy

App: 日本旅日記 - 旅の日記としおり (Nihon Tabi Nikki) / Last updated: November 14, 2026

misoclub (“we,” “us,” or “our”) sets out below how it handles user information in “日本旅日記 - 旅の日記としおり (Nihon Tabi Nikki)” (the “App”).

1. Information We Collect

The App stores the content you enter or record (travel destinations, dates, photos, notes, tags, travel itineraries, nickname, and the like) only on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or obtain this information.

2. Personal Information

No account registration is required to use the App. We do not collect your name or email address through the App. See “3. Communications” for how location is used in Find Accommodation and “5. Provision to Third Parties” for how information is handled when ads are served.

3. Communications

The App does not send your travel records (diaries, photos, itineraries, etc.) to any server.

However, the following features communicate over the internet. In every case, we do not obtain your travel records.

  • “Ads”: The App uses Google AdMob to display ads and communicates with Google’s services to do so.
  • “Find Accommodation”: When you search for lodging, the search conditions you enter (area, dates, number of guests, etc.) are sent to Rakuten Travel (Rakuten Group, Inc.). If you grant location access and search for nearby lodging, the location needed for the search is sent to Rakuten Travel through a relay server.
  • “In-app browser”: When you open a link, such as a place to stop by, communication occurs in order to display that page.

4. Device Permissions

We may use access to your photos/storage for the purpose of saving shareable images to your device. This is used solely to save images; we do not obtain your photos or similar data.

5. Provision to Third Parties

We do not share your travel records with third parties. Google may collect and use information when it serves ads. For details, see the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

6. Deleting Data

You can delete stored records at any time yourself, using the App’s “Reset all data” function or by uninstalling the App.

7. Children’s Privacy

If a child uses the App, see “3. Communications” and “5. Provision to Third Parties” for how location and advertising information are handled.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Policy as necessary. If there are significant changes, we will notify you through app updates or similar means.
